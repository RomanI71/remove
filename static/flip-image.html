Flip Image Online – Mirror Photos Instantly

Use the **Flip Image Tool** to easily create a mirror image of your photos. Whether you need to flip a selfie horizontally to fix its orientation or flip a photo vertically, our free online tool handles the job instantly while preserving image quality and transparency.

Why Use Our Image Flipper?

  • **Preserve Transparency:** Keeps PNG and WEBP image transparency intact upon saving.
  • **Client-Side Processing:** All flipping happens in your browser for maximum speed and privacy.
  • **Simple Controls:** Quick buttons for horizontal and vertical flipping.
  • **High Quality:** No loss of quality during the transformation.

How to Flip an Image?

  1. Click **Select Image** to upload your photo (JPG, PNG, or WEBP).
  2. Click **Flip Horizontal** to create a mirror image.
  3. Click **Flip Vertical** to flip it upside down.
  4. Click **Save** to download your new image.
