Rotate Image Online – Free Image Rotation Tool

Easily **rotate your images** with TopImgTools. Our free online tool allows you to rotate photos by fixed degrees (90°, 180°, 270°) or adjust to a custom angle. Perfect for fixing camera orientation or creating unique effects.

Key Features

  • **Preserves Quality:** Uses PNG output for high quality and transparency support.
  • **Client-Side Processing:** Images are rotated securely in your browser—no uploads required.
  • **Easy Controls:** Quick buttons for common rotations (90°, 180°) and a slider for fine-tuning.
  • **Flip Functionality:** Quickly flip images horizontally or vertically.

How to Rotate an Image?

  1. Upload your JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
  2. Use the **90° CW** or **90° CCW** buttons for quick adjustments.
  3. Use the **Custom Angle** slider for precise rotation.
  4. Click **Download PNG** to save your rotated image.
