Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does this face blur tool store my images?

Absolutely not. We prioritize user security. Since we use client-side technology, the image never leaves your computer or phone. It is processed in your browser's memory and wiped as soon as you close the tab.

Can I control how blurry the faces look?

Yes. We provide an adjustable intensity slider. You can choose a subtle "soft focus" effect or a strong "pixelated" or Gaussian blur to ensure the person is completely unrecognizable.

Does it work on photos where faces are turned sideways?

Our AI is trained to recognize faces from various angles, including profiles (side views) and tilted heads. However, if a face is completely covered or turned away from the camera, the AI might skip it to preserve the rest of the image.

Is there a limit to how many photos I can edit?

No. This tool is completely free and unlimited. You can process as many images as you need for your projects without any watermarks or hidden costs.
