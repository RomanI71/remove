Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Will I lose image quality after cropping?

A: No. Our tool does not add unnecessary compression. It simply trims the pixels outside your selection, keeping the original resolution of the remaining area intact.

Q: Can I crop PNG images with transparent backgrounds?

A: Absolutely! Our cropper fully supports transparency. Your cropped PNG will retain its transparent properties perfectly.

Q: Is it safe to use for sensitive photos?

A: Yes. Your privacy is our priority. Since all the processing is done via your browser (client-side), your images are never sent to our servers. Your data stays on your device.

Q: Do I need to register or create an account?

A: No registration is required. Our Free Online Image Cropper is open for everyone to use, anytime, without any barriers.
