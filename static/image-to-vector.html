Why Use an Image to Vector Converter?

The primary difference between raster (JPG/PNG) and vector (SVG/EPS) files is how they are constructed. Raster images are made of pixels, while vectors are made of mathematical paths. Here is why converting to vector is essential:

  • Unlimited Scalability: Unlike raster images, vectors do not lose quality when scaled. You can enlarge a vector file to the size of a billboard or shrink it for a business card without any blurriness.
  • Perfect for Branding: Logos should always be in vector format. Converting your old JPG logo to vector ensures your brand looks sharp across all media.
  • Print-Ready Graphics: Professional printers require vector files for clear and crisp results on t-shirts, banners, and brochures.
  • Ease of Editing: Once converted, vector paths can be easily modified in software like Adobe Illustrator or Inkscape.
  • No Software Needed: Our tool eliminates the need for expensive software like Photoshop. You can achieve professional results online and for free.
