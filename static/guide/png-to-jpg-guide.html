PNG to JPG Converter Guide

The **PNG to JPG Converter** tool allows you to transform PNG images into the space-saving JPG format quickly and easily. This is ideal for optimizing images for websites, emails, or storage, as JPG files are generally much smaller.

1. Upload Your PNG Image(s)

Start by uploading the PNG file(s) you wish to convert. The tool supports both single-file and batch conversions.

Upload Options:

  • Select Image: Click the blue "Select Image" button to browse your device.
  • Drag & Drop: Drag your PNG file(s) directly into the dashed upload area.
  • Note: Since JPG format does not support transparency, any transparent areas in your PNG image will be automatically filled with white upon conversion.

2. Set JPG Quality

Before conversion, you have the option to set the output quality of the resulting JPG file. This directly impacts the file size.

Quality Slider:

  • Use the **Quality Slider** to choose a compression level, typically from 1 to 100.
  • **High Quality (90-100):** Provides the best visual fidelity, resulting in a larger file size. Use this for high-resolution photography.
  • **Medium Quality (70-89):** A good balance between visual quality and file size reduction. Ideal for general web use.
  • **Lower Quality (Below 70):** Results in maximum file size reduction, but noticeable visual artifacts may appear.

We recommend a quality setting of 80-90 for the best balance of speed and appearance.

3. Convert and Download

Once your PNG file(s) are uploaded and the quality level is set, the conversion process is simple.

Finalizing the Conversion:

  • Click the blue **"Convert to JPG"** button. The conversion is performed instantly on the client side.
  • A **"Download"** button will appear for each converted JPG file, or a single ZIP file download for batch conversions.
  • Click **"Download"** to save your new JPG file(s).
