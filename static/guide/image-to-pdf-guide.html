Image to PDF Converter Tutorial

The **Image to PDF** tool is the easiest way to compile your photos into a single, shareable PDF document. It supports modern formats like **HEIC**, **WEBP**, as well as standard JPG and PNG, all processed securely within your browser.

1. Select Your Images

Choose the images you want to combine into a document.

Upload Options:

  • Drag & Drop: Drag your image files directly into the upload box.
  • Browse: Click the "Select Files" button to choose from your device.
  • Supported Formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, HEIC, and HEIF.
  • Size Limit: Each file should be under 25MB.

Previews of your selected images will appear in a grid instantly.

2. Review & Generate

Once your images are loaded, you can review them before creating the PDF.

Creating the Document:

  • Review: Check the preview grid to ensure all your images are present.
  • Generate: Click the **"Generate PDF"** button.
  • Conversion: The tool will automatically convert HEIC/HEIF files to JPG and fit all images to PDF pages.

A status message will indicate when the PDF is being generated.

3. Download PDF

Your document is ready to be saved.

Final Step:

  • The browser will automatically prompt you to save the file named **images.pdf**.
  • You can now share your single PDF file instead of sending multiple image attachments.
  • To start over, simply click the **"Clear"** button.

Why use Image to PDF?

Sending multiple images via email can be messy and often exceeds attachment limits. Converting them to a single PDF ensures the recipient sees the images in the correct order, in a single file that is easy to print and view on any device.
