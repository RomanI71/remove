Advanced Meme Generator Tutorial

The **Advanced Meme Generator** is a fast and flexible tool for creating memes directly in your browser. Upload an image, add customizable text, and position it anywhere to create the perfect meme.

1. Upload Your Image

Start by choosing the base image for your meme.

Upload Options:

  • Drag & Drop: Simply drag an image file into the upload area.
  • Select Image: Click the button to browse your device for a file.
  • Supported Formats: Works with JPG, PNG, WebP, and more.

The image will appear on the canvas immediately after upload.

2. Add & Customize Text

Make your meme funny or informative with custom text.

Editing Features:

  • Top & Bottom Text: Type into the input fields to add text. It automatically converts to uppercase for the classic meme look.
  • Drag & Drop: Click and drag the text directly on the preview canvas to position it anywhere.
  • Colors: Change the text color for both top and bottom lines independently.
  • Font Size & Rotation: Use the sliders to adjust the size and angle of your text.

3. Generate & Download

Finalize your creation and save it.

Saving Your Meme:

  • Click **"Generate"** to refresh the preview with your latest changes.
  • Click **"Download"** to save the final meme as a high-quality JPG image.

Why use this Meme Generator?

Unlike basic generators that lock text to the top and bottom, this tool offers **drag-and-drop positioning**, **rotation**, and **color customization**, giving you complete creative freedom.
