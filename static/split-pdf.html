Split PDF Online – Extract Pages & Separate Documents Free

Take control of your documents. Split PDF files, extract specific pages, or separate one large document into several smaller files instantly. Fast, free, and secure.

Why Do You Need a PDF Splitter?

PDFs are the standard for sharing documents, but they can often be unwieldy. You might receive a 100-page contract when you only need to sign the last page, or a scanned bundle of receipts when you only need to expense one.

Our free PDF Splitter allows you to declutter your digital life. By removing unnecessary pages, you not only make your files more organized but also significantly reduce PDF file size, making them easier to email, upload, and store.

Key Features

  • Precise Page Extraction: You have full control. Extract a single page, a specific range (e.g., pages 5-10), or non-consecutive pages (e.g., pages 3, 7, and 12).
  • Zero Quality Loss: When you separate a PDF, the text sharpness, formatting, and image resolution of the extracted pages remain exactly the same as the original.
  • 100% Secure & Private: We value your privacy. Your files are processed securely, and because we don't store your data, your sensitive documents remain yours alone.
  • No Installation Required: Forget downloading heavy software like Adobe Acrobat. Our tool works entirely in your web browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, and Android.

Real-World Use Cases

Who benefits from splitting PDFs?

  • Students & Researchers: Extract just the relevant chapter from a massive digital textbook or research paper to save space on your tablet.
  • Business Professionals: Separate a single invoice or receipt from a scanned batch to send to the accounting department.
  • Legal & HR: Remove confidential pages from a report before sharing the rest of the document with external clients or employees.

How to Split PDF Files Online

Separating your PDF pages is a simple 4-step process:

  1. Upload Your PDF: Drag and drop your file into the box or click to select it from your device storage.
  2. Select Your Pages: Enter the page numbers you wish to keep. You can use hyphens for ranges (e.g., "1-5") or commas for individual pages (e.g., "1, 3, 5").
  3. Process the File: Click the "Split PDF" button. The tool will instantly create a new document containing only the pages you selected.
  4. Download: Save your new, streamlined PDF file to your device immediately.
