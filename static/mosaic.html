What is a Photo Mosaic?

A photo mosaic (sometimes called a photographic mosaic) is a large, central picture—often referred to as the "master photo"—that has been recreated using hundreds or even thousands of much smaller, individual photos called "tiles." When viewed from a distance, the primary image is crystal clear. However, as you step closer or zoom in, you discover that the primary image is actually composed of an intricate grid of entirely different pictures. It is a fantastic way to tell a multi-layered visual story.

Historically, creating these required heavy, expensive software like Photoshop alongside specialized plugins. Today, our entirely web-based Mosaic Maker allows anyone to generate these massive, complex visual compositions in seconds with zero technical experience required.

If you intend to print your final creation on a large canvas or poster board, achieving the correct resolution is critical. Because our tool allows you to download uncompressed PNG files with extreme tile resolutions, the resulting file sizes can be massive (often exceeding 4000x4000 pixels). This makes them perfect for 300 DPI (Dots Per Inch) professional printing. When setting up for print, we highly recommend setting the grid density to 120 or higher and the Tile Resolution to 'High' or 'Extreme'. This ensures that when people walk up close to the printed canvas, the tiny photos are as clear as day.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How many source tiles should I upload for the best results?

While the generator will work with as few as 5 photos (it will intelligently repeat them), we strongly recommend uploading between 50 to 200 distinct photos. A larger pool of images gives the algorithm a wider variety of colors and brightness levels to choose from, resulting in a much more accurate recreation of your master image.

My generated image is too large / crashing my browser. What do I do?

If you select "Extreme" for both Density and Tile Resolution, the browser attempts to draw a massive image that can consume a lot of RAM. If you are on an older computer or a mobile device, try lowering the Tile Resolution to "Medium" or reducing the Density to "80 Columns" for a smoother experience.

What does the "Overlay Blending" slider actually do?

A true mosaic relies strictly on the colors of the tile photos to build the image. However, if your tile photos don't have the exact right colors, the master image can become hard to recognize. The blending slider gently superimposes the original master photo over the top of the grid (acting as a semi-transparent filter), which helps to dramatically define the shapes and shadows of the main subject.

Are my images safe?

Absolutely. The TopImgTools Mosaic Generator is an entirely front-end application. That means the mathematical processing that stitches your photos together happens inside the RAM of your own device. Your images are never transmitted across the internet to our servers, ensuring absolute privacy for your personal files.
