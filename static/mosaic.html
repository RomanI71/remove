What is a Photo Mosaic?

A photo mosaic is a single image composed of hundreds or even thousands of smaller, individual photos. From a distance, you see the "master image," but as you zoom in, the intricate details of the smaller tiles become visible. This visual storytelling technique is perfect for celebrating anniversaries, company milestones, or creating unique social media content.

With our free mosaic generator, you don't need to be a Photoshop expert. Our algorithm does the heavy lifting, analyzing the colors and shapes of your source photos to build a perfect reconstruction of your main image.

Pro-Tips for Perfect Mosaics

For the best results, keep these tips in mind:

  • High Contrast: Master images with high contrast (bright lights and deep shadows) tend to look better as mosaics.
  • Simple Shapes: Portraits or single-subject photos work better than complex, busy landscapes.
  • Tile Variety: If you are using multiple photos for tiles, try to use images with a wide variety of colors to help the algorithm match the master image accurately.
