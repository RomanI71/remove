Free Online Image Upscaler – Enlarge Photos Without Losing Quality

Need to make a small image bigger but worried about it becoming blurry or pixelated? Our Online Image Upscaler uses smart browser-based rendering to resize and enlarge your pictures instantly. Whether it's a small logo, a low-resolution photo, or digital art, you can boost its size up to 8x directly from your browser.

How to Upscale an Image?

  1. Upload: Drag and drop your small image into the workspace.
  2. Choose Scale: Select how much larger you want the image (2x, 4x, etc.).
  3. Select Quality: Choose "Smooth" for photos or "Pixelated" for Pixel Art/Logos.
  4. Download: Click process and download your high-resolution image instantly.
