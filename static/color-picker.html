How to Extract Color Codes from an Image?

Getting started is easy. Follow these four simple steps to master your color selection:

  1. Upload Your Source: Drag and drop your image (JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF) into the workspace. You can also click to browse your local files.
  2. Precision Navigation: Use the zoom slider or the interactive magnifier lens to hover over the image. This allows you to isolate individual pixels, ensuring you don't pick the wrong shade in high-detail areas.
  3. Sample the Color: Once you've found the perfect spot, click to lock in the selection. The tool will instantly generate a preview of the color and its corresponding data.
  4. Copy & Implement: To obtain the RGB values for design applications such as Adobe XD, Canva, or Figma, or the HEX code for CSS/HTML, use the one-click copy buttons.

We support all common web formats, including JPEG, PNG, WebP, and even GIFs. Whether it's a high-res photograph or a small icon, the tool works perfectly.
