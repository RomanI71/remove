Why Image Compression Matters for SEO

Search engines like Google now use Core Web Vitals as a primary ranking factor. One of the most critical metrics is "Largest Contentful Paint" (LCP), which measures how quickly the main content of a page loads. Frequently, the culprit behind a slow LCP is a massive, uncompressed hero image or product photo.

By using an online photo compressor, you can:

  • Improve Page Load Speed: Faster sites provide a better user experience and keep visitors from clicking away.
  • Lower Bounce Rates: Users are less likely to leave a site that loads instantly.
  • Conserve Storage & Bandwidth: Smaller files save data for your mobile users and use less space on your server.
  • Boost Mobile Performance: Optimized images are crucial for users on 4G/5G connections where data speeds can vary.
