Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will the JPG still be animated?

A: No. JPG is a static image format. The converter will capture the first frame of your GIF and turn it into a high-quality still photo.

Q: Does this work for transparent GIFs?

A: Yes! However, since JPG does not support transparency, any transparent areas will automatically be filled with a solid white background.

Q: Is there a limit to the file size I can upload?

A: We support most standard GIF sizes. For the best experience on mobile, we recommend files under 20MB.
