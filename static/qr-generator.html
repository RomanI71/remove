Professional Online QR Code Generator with Frames

In the digital age, Quick Response (QR) codes are the bridge between the physical and digital worlds. Whether you are a business owner sharing a menu, a teacher distributing notes, or an influencer growing your following, our Custom QR Code Generator is the perfect tool for you. Unlike basic generators, we offer stylish frames and customization options to make your codes stand out.

At topimgtools.com, we prioritize speed and privacy. Our tool runs entirely in your browser using advanced JavaScript libraries. This means you can create high-resolution QR codes without signing up, installing software, or risking your data privacy.

Why Choose Our QR Designer?

  • ✔ Creative Frames: Don't settle for boring black squares. Use our "Polaroid" frame for events, "Speech Bubble" for messages, or "Phone" frame for app links.
  • ✔ High Quality Formats: Download in PNG for transparency, JPG for general use, or PDF for printing.

How to Create a Custom QR Code

  1. Enter Content: Type your Website URL, text, email, or phone number in the top input box.
  2. Customize: Select a frame from the sidebar (e.g., Polaroid, Ribbon) and choose a color that matches your brand.
  3. Preview: Watch the live preview update instantly as you make changes.
  4. Download: Select your preferred format (PNG/JPG/PDF) and click "Download File".

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do these QR codes expire?

A: No. These are static QR codes. Once generated, they will work forever as long as the link you put inside them is valid.

Q: Can I scan these with my phone?

A: Yes! Our generator creates standard QR codes compatible with all iPhone and Android camera apps.
