Why Is PDF Better Than Standard Images?

It's crucial to comprehend the benefits of converting your photos to PDF before delving into the features. Unlike standard image formats (like JPG or PNG), a PDF (Portable Document Format) is designed to look exactly the same on any device—be it a smartphone, tablet, or desktop computer. By converting your images, you ensure that the formatting, quality, and sequence of your documents remain intact, preventing compatibility issues when sharing files via email or messaging apps.
