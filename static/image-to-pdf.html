Why Is PDF Better Than Standard Images?

Before diving into the features, it is important to understand why converting your images to PDF is a smart move. Unlike standard image formats (like JPG or PNG), a PDF (Portable Document Format) is designed to look exactly the same on any device—be it a smartphone, tablet, or desktop computer. By converting your images, you ensure that the formatting, quality, and sequence of your documents remain intact, preventing compatibility issues when sharing files via email or messaging apps.
