Reduce PDF Size Online: Effective & Secure

Are you struggling with large PDF documents that are too big to email? TopImgTools PDF Compressor is the solution. Our tool helps you reduce PDF file size by optimizing the contents of your document to make it lightweight and shareable.

Why Use TopImgTools PDF Compressor?

  • 100% Privacy First: Your files never touch our servers. Compression happens entirely inside your web browser.
  • Effective Rasterization: To guarantee a smaller size, we convert complex PDF pages into optimized images. Note: This flattens the document, making text non-selectable, but ensures maximum size reduction.
  • No Installation Required: Skip the bulky software. Works on any device with a browser.

How to Compress Your PDF?

  1. Upload: Drag your PDF into the box above.
  2. Select Quality: Choose your preferred compression level.
  3. Process: Click "Compress PDF" to start the local optimization.
